Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about notifications, privacy, and device compatibility.

Do alarms ring if the app is completely closed or killed? ▾
Yes! DoseDay registers exact one-shot alarms with Android's system AlarmManager using SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM and USE_EXACT_ALARM. Even if you swipe the app away from recent tasks, the system wakes the device and triggers the high-priority reminder with sound and vibration at the exact minute.
How does the Monday-to-Sunday weekly strip work? ▾
The schedule calendar is structured as a full 7-day Monday through Sunday calendar week. When you swipe left/right or tap the previous/next chevrons, the view restarts smoothly with the next or previous week's Monday-to-Sunday block. You can also tap the Today button at any time to return to today's active date.
Is my health and medication data private? ▾
100% private. All medication names, dosage quantities, refill logs, and profile records are stored exclusively in your device's local encrypted SQLite database. There are no remote servers, no user accounts required, and no tracking scripts.
Tips for Vivo, Xiaomi, Oppo, or Samsung phones? ▾
Some Android manufacturers enable aggressive battery savers. For maximum reliability, go to your phone's Settings > Apps > DoseDay > Battery and set it to "Unrestricted" or "No restrictions", and ensure "Alarms & Reminders" permission is allowed.
