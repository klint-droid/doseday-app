FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DoseDay's Tarsi-inspired interface, offline privacy, and exact alarms.

Can I mark a dose as Taken or Snooze it directly from the notification? ▾
Yes! Our upgraded Notification Engine v6 includes interactive action buttons (✓ Take Dose, ⏰ Snooze 10m, and ✕ Skip) directly on the system notification card, and automatically updates your pill stock and streak.
Do alarms ring even when the app is completely closed? ▾
Yes. DoseDay uses Android's hardware-backed AlarmManager with exactAllowWhileIdle and high-priority alarm audio channels so reminders fire on the exact minute even if the app is swiped away.
Does DoseDay require an account or internet connection? ▾
Never. Like Tarsi, DoseDay is 100% offline-first. All your prescriptions, dose history, and refill thresholds stay strictly on your device in local SQLite storage.
